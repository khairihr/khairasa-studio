Website questions

What business owners ask

How long does a website or system take?

A landing page usually takes 1 to 2 weeks. A website with a system (reservations, bookings, catalog) takes around 3 to 6 weeks, depending on features. We give a firm estimate in the proposal after we talk.

Do I get full access to the source code and domain?

Yes. After handover, full access to the code, domain, and hosting accounts is yours. Nothing is locked, and there is no forced dependency on us.

Will it just be a template?

No. Design is custom (Figma-led) and the system is real engineering, built by a lead engineer who ships production software daily. You can click the live demos above to see the quality.

Can you handle payments and large data?

Yes, on request. We integrate payments (QRIS, virtual accounts) through trusted providers, and the engineering background is specifically in billing, APIs, and performance on million-row datasets.

How do deposits and payments work?

Usually a deposit up front to start, then the balance on handover. Larger projects can be split into milestone payments. Everything is discussed openly, with no hidden costs.

Is there support after launch?

Yes. There is a support period after launch, plus optional monthly maintenance plans with a defined response SLA. See our Service & SLA page.
