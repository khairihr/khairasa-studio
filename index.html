Questions people often ask

Still unsure? That is fair.

You are new, why should I trust you?

The studio is new, but the people are not. You can check our track record directly: thousands of code contributions in production systems, real certifications, and real achievements. On top of that, there is a revision guarantee and a deposit refund if the initial concept does not fit.

How is a wedding content creator different from a regular videographer?

A videographer focuses on formal, long-form cinematic documentation. We focus on social-media content: short, candid, fast reels (same-day edits), ready to post on Instagram and TikTok. The two complement each other, they do not replace one another.

How long does a website or system take?

A landing page usually takes 1 to 2 weeks. A website with a system (reservations, bookings, catalog) takes around 3 to 6 weeks, depending on the features. We give a firm estimate in the proposal after we talk.

How long until the wedding reels are ready?

A teaser can be ready the same day (same-day edit). The full reels are delivered within 24 to 48 hours, while the moment is still fresh to share.

Do I get full access to the source code and domain?

Yes. After handover, full access to the code, domain, and hosting accounts is yours. Nothing is locked, and there is no forced dependency on us.

How do deposits and payments work?

Usually a deposit up front to secure the schedule, then the balance on handover. The scheme can be adjusted and is discussed openly up front, with no hidden costs.

Is there support after the website launches?

Yes. We provide a support period after launch for fixes and questions. For long-term maintenance, that can be arranged separately.

What areas do you cover for weddings?

We are based in Cirebon and Bandung, and open to nearby areas and other cities. Tell us your location on WhatsApp and we will sort out the logistics.
