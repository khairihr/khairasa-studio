Service policy

What a project includes

Scope & deliverables

Every project starts with a written proposal so we both agree on the same thing before any work begins.

  • A clear list of pages, features, and deliverables
  • A timeline with milestones and an honest estimate
  • Design and build done in stages, with regular updates
  • Work outside the agreed scope is quoted separately before we start it, never billed by surprise

Revisions

We refine the work until it matches the brief we agreed on.

  • Revisions within the agreed scope are included
  • Each milestone has a review step so feedback comes early, not all at the end
  • A brand-new direction after approval counts as new scope and is quoted first

Typical timelines

  • Landing page or company profile: about 1 to 2 weeks
  • Website with a system (reservation, booking, catalog): about 3 to 6 weeks
  • Custom system: scoped per project, set in the proposal
  • Timelines assume content and feedback arrive on time from your side

Payment & deposit

  • A deposit up front to secure your slot and start work
  • The balance on handover, before the site goes fully live
  • Larger projects can be split into milestone payments
  • Prices are quoted in Rupiah, with an approximate USD figure for reference. No hidden costs

Ownership & handover

When the project is done and settled, it is fully yours.

  • You own the source code, the domain, and the hosting accounts
  • Full access is handed over to you, with a short walkthrough
  • No lock-in. You are free to host, edit, or move it anywhere

Support SLA

How fast we respond

For clients on an active support or maintenance plan. We target these response times, backed by real monitoring.

Critical

Same business day

Site down, checkout broken, or anything blocking your customers. We respond first and fix as the priority.

High

Within 1 business day

A major feature is broken but the site still runs. We schedule and resolve it quickly.

Normal

2 to 3 business days

Minor bugs, small change requests, and questions. Handled in order.

Support hours & uptime

  • Support hours: Monday to Saturday, 09:00 to 18:00 WIB (UTC+7)
  • Uptime depends on your hosting provider. We set up monitoring so problems are caught early and responded to, not discovered by your customers
  • We aim for high availability and keep an eye on performance, but we do not promise a hard uptime percentage we cannot control end to end
  • Response time means time to first response and a plan, not always instant resolution

Wedding service

Wedding terms

Booking & delivery

  • A deposit locks your date. Dates are first come, first served

  • One round of revision on the edit is included

Good to know

What is not included

Out of scope & third-party costs

To keep pricing honest, these sit outside a project or maintenance fee and are billed by the relevant provider, or quoted separately:

  • Hosting, domain registration and renewal, and paid plugins or licenses
  • Payment gateway fees, and WhatsApp Business API access (set up via a provider, on request)
  • Copywriting, professional photography, or stock assets, unless agreed in the scope
  • Major new features added after sign-off, which are quoted as new work
