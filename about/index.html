About the studio

Two people, two crafts, one studio

Khairasa Studio is small on purpose. The people you talk to are the people who do the work. One side makes things that are beautiful to watch, the other makes systems that are reliable to run.

Our story

The name says it best

Khairasa comes from our names, Khairi and Zalza, and it also reads like "khair" and "rasa": goodness and feeling. That is what we set out to make. Work that is built right, and work that feels cared for.

We started Khairasa because we kept seeing the same two gaps. Websites that look pretty but break, or quietly do nothing. And important moments that deserve to be remembered, captured in a way that feels stiff instead of alive. One of us spends every day building production software. The other spends every day reading rooms, people, and the flow of an event. Put together, that is a studio that can both build the thing and make the whole experience feel good.

We are a new studio, and we are honest about that. But we are not new to the work. You can check our track record yourself, and we put our names and faces on everything we ship.
