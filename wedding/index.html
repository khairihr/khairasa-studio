Wedding questions

The things couples ask

How is this different from a regular wedding videographer?

A videographer focuses on formal, long-form cinematic documentation. We focus on social-media content: short, candid, fast reels (same-day edits), ready to post on Instagram and TikTok. The two complement each other, they do not replace one another.

How long until we get our reels?

A teaser can be ready the same day. The full reels are delivered within 24 to 48 hours, while the moment is still fresh to share.

What areas do you cover? Can you travel?

We are based in Cirebon and Bandung and happily travel to nearby areas and other cities. Out-of-town coverage may add a transport cost, agreed up front. Tell us your venue and we will sort the logistics.

What if it rains or the schedule slips?

Outdoor weddings and shifting timelines are normal, and an event-planning background means we plan for them. We adapt on the day and still deliver your teaser and reels on time.

You are a new studio, why should we trust you?

The studio is new, but the people are not. Your day is run by a Cum Laude tourism graduate and BNSP-certified event planner. We also send real sample reels on WhatsApp so you see the style before you commit.
